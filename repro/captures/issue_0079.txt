## 2. What the reporter described

When multiple MCPClient instances are cleaned up out of order, the transport's cancel scope management across tasks fails, throwing CancelledError: Cancelled by cancel scope

## 3. What we captured


## 4. Why it happens

When multiple MCPClient instances are cleaned up out of order across tasks, the transport's cancel scope ownership gets confused and `CancelledError: Cancelled by cancel scope` is thrown. The source is the Client's session management, exposed to the caller as a bare exception — a caller-exception.
