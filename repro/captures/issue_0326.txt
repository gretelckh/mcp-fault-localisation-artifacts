## 4. Why it happens

When `env` is set to non-None (e.g. `{}`), the child process loses PATH, npx fails to start, and the SDK only throws `MCPError: Connection closed` at `jsonrpc_dispatcher.send_raw_request`, swallowing the real cause (child process startup failure). The symptom is reproducible, and the root cause is that env overrides the entire environment — a caller-exception.
