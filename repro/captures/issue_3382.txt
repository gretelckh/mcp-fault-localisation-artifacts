## 4. Why it happens

`OAuthClientProvider.async_auth_flow` holds the lock with `async with self.context.lock` across `yield`; when httpx closes the flow in another task, releasing the lock fails, the lock is permanently poisoned, and subsequent requests throw `RuntimeError: Attempted to acquire an already held Lock`. This is a caller-exception (authentication lock lifecycle defect).
