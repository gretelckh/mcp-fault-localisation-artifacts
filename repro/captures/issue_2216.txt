## 2. What the reporter described

validate_scope 在客户端未注册任何 scope（None）时，会误拒绝任何请求：validate_scope(None, 'read') 抛 InvalidScopeError: Client was not registered with scope read

## 3. What we captured

Captured in our run (evidence tier A).

```
validate_scope(scope=None, req='read') -> RAISED InvalidScopeError: Client was not registered with scope read
```

## 4. Why it happens

`validate_scope` 在客户端未注册任何 scope（None）时误拒绝任何请求：`validate_scope(None, 'read')` 抛 `InvalidScopeError: Client was not registered with scope read`。无 required scopes 时应放行，此逻辑缺陷导致正常请求被拒，属 single-layer 逻辑缺陷。
