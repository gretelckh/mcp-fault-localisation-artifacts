## 4. Why it happens

When the server exposes no tools/resources/prompts, `ClientSessionGroup._aggregate_components` executes `del self._session_exit_stacks[session]` on an unregistered session, throwing `KeyError: <ClientSession object>` (session_group.py:420). This is a caller-exception (empty component registration path defect).
