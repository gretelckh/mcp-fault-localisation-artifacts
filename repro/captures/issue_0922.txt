## 2. What the reporter described

## 4. Why it happens

When multiple client sessions are created in a task group and closed in separate tasks, cancel scope ownership gets confused and `RuntimeError: Attempted to exit cancel scope in a different task than it was entered in` is thrown. Exposed to the caller as a bare exception — a caller-exception.
